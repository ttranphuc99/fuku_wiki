Week 2 — System Design for Payments

Highest-leverage week. Practice designing out loud, 45 minutes each, narrating trade-offs like someone who's owned production incidents. For every design, explicitly state the business requirement behind each technical choice — that's your differentiator.

WK2-D01Design a payment gatewaystudy guide →
Client → auth → risk/fraud check → ledger write → settlement, with idempotency and retry-safety.

Cover: consistency model, failure modes, idempotency, what you'd alert on, and how you'd roll it out safely (canary, feature flag, dark launch).

WK2-D02Design a ledger / reconciliation systemstudy guide →
Guarantee no double-charge or lost transaction across service failures.

Think about write-ahead logs, exactly-once vs at-least-once delivery, and reconciliation jobs as a safety net, not the primary guarantee.

WK2-D03Design a high-availability transaction processorstudy guide →
Handling downstream bank/partner outages gracefully.

Bring in bulkheads and circuit breakers here — tie the pattern back to a concrete partner-outage scenario.

WK2-D04Design a fraud/risk scoring pipelinestudy guide →
Sync vs async scoring, and the latency budget trade-off between the two.
